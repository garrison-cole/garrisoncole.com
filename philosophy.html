A business grows in order.

Most owners pay to fix layer two or three while layer one is cracked. Here's the order I check them in.

The four layers

bottom up ↑

bottom up

  1. What it looks like broken

    • You quote by the hour or by the day.
    • You win when you're the cheapest bid and lose when you're not.
    • You flinch when someone says they're getting three bids.

    What I do about it

    Price on the result the customer is buying. First we put the real cost of a job on paper, so the number holds up when someone pushes on it.

    The number that tells you

    Close rate at full price

  2. What it looks like broken

    • Shared leads from Angi or HomeAdvisor that three other companies also got.
    • Feast or famine, month to month.
    • "We get most of our work from referrals." Great until a slow month shows up.

    What I do about it

    Google Search first, for the people typing your trade and your town right now. Meta once the tracking is clean, for the before-and-after work that sells itself. Warm before cold.

    The number that tells you

    Cost per lead

  3. What it looks like broken

    • The phone goes to voicemail at 4pm because you're on a job.
    • Quotes go out and nobody calls back.
    • You're the only one who can close.
    • The price talk falls apart the moment a third bid shows up.

    What I do about it

    Speed to lead, follow-up that runs without you, and quotes built to get a yes. For some clients I take the sales calls myself. For the rest I coach the owner through them.

    The number that tells you

    Close rate and cost per booked job

  4. What it looks like broken

    • A great spring and a tight January.
    • No idea what last month's jobs cost after labor, materials and fuel.

    What I do about it

    The four numbers on one page, looked at every week. A slow-season plan that starts before spring ends. And a close rate you can predict before anybody talks about spending more.

    The number that tells you

    Revenue per crew per week

Rules I work by.

  • Warm before cold.
  • Google Search first for intent and lead quality. Meta once the tracking is clean.
  • A new campaign stays paused until the pixel, the call tracking and the follow-up are wired and checked.
  • Find a predictable close rate before scaling spend.
  • You pay ad spend directly and see every dollar.
  • Record the sales calls and listen back.
  • Follow up the same day.

What I won't do.

  • Advertise work that isn't there yet.
  • Send you a report about impressions.
  • Put a price on a website.
  • Promise a number before I've seen the business.
