Hi, I'm Avery.

Before I had training nailed, I scoured the internet for the perfect split. The right exercises. I followed apps, online programs and workouts, tested them against each other and copied the people I wanted to look like.

It left me with a body I didn't recognize, burnout through the roof and nowhere near my goal.

What I know now? None of them were right for me. I never once asked what was going on in MY life and what my body actually needed because of it. Let me save you yearsssss of that

Your plan should change when you get stronger. When you add new movement. When you're pregnant, postpartum or breastfeeding. When your job, your stress and your day to day change.

So here's what I believe, and what every coach on my team coaches by:

  • You can't hate yourself into your dream body.
  • You can't build your dream body inside a life you hate.
  • Every coach is selling you more rigidity. I think they're wrong. You need more SPECIFICITY.
  • Rigidity doesn't automatically mean discipline. Neither does never telling yourself no. The skill is knowing what you say yes and no to, and when.
  • Life is supposed to be fun. Eat out. Travel. Have the babies. Hit the huge career goals. Get your bag AND a body that matches your effort everywhere else.
  • My health and my success were never separate. The energy and confidence to build my business came from showing up for my body first.
  • Your daughters and future daughters deserve to feel good around mirrors and food. We're breaking the cycle here.
